About Global Harlem

Global Harlem is a media company built on the Harlem name and spirit. We listen to communities, measure trust, and publish what we learn as community intelligence for residents, partners, and organizations. There are Harlems all over the world; they just go by different names.

Published journalism.

Reporting and essays published across community and national outlets, grounded in listening before publishing.

Empowering NYC's Immigrant Communities Through Mental Health

NepYork, June 2026. Coverage of the Minority Empowerment Network's second annual community wellness summit at NYC Health + Hospitals/Elmhurst, where speakers addressed mental health stigma and access gaps in Nepali, South Asian, and immigrant communities across Queens.

The Winter People of Governors Island Keep it Running

New York City News Service, December 2025. A photo-driven feature on the construction workers and ferry crews who keep Governors Island running through the off-season, when the crowds are gone and the island's own weather takes over.

Belonging Without Assimilation

We Are One Humanity, July 2026. An essay revisiting Uday Dandavate's "A Shared Destiny on Borrowed Land," drawing on personal experience in corporate multicultural marketing to argue that institutions confuse the appearance of diversity with the infrastructure of genuine belonging.
